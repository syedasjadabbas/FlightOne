## Executive summary

**Atriis (Atriis Technologies Ltd.)** is a **managed corporate-travel SaaS** vendor. It sells a **shared platform** used by **Travel Management Companies (TMCs)** and their **corporate clients** (travel managers + travelers) on the same stack — unifying **GDS, NDC, direct, hotel, and rail** content to reduce **off-platform booking (“leakage”)**.

| Lens | Snapshot |
|---|---|
| Category | TMC enablement / corporate OBT / NDC aggregation |
| Buyers | Mid–large TMCs (primary) + corporates (often via TMC white-label) |
| Core narrative | *Gain control. Overcome leakage. Transform travel management.* |
| Differentiator | Shared agent + traveler UI; IATA NDC Level 4; live GDS↔NDC compare; “Global Shared Marketplace” |
| GTM | Enterprise sales-led; announce-heavy newsroom; Europe-weighted TMC land-grab |
| Capital | Appears **bootstrapped / no disclosed VC** (third-party databases) |
| Pricing | **Not public** — setup + subscription/transaction hybrid (trade sources) |
| FlightOne fit | **Not a near-term vendor match** (corporate/GDS/Europe-centric). Strong **product-strategy case study** (leakage, shared agent/customer view, content aggregation). |

## 1. Company overview

### What they are
Atriis builds technology for **managed business travel**: multi-channel content + agent desktop + corporate online booking tool (OBT) + post-booking service, framed as saving the **TMC model** from disintermediation by consumer OTAs, airline.com, and direct-to-corporate challengers.

### Who they serve
1. **TMCs** — white-label / branded booking + agent ops platform they deploy to corporate clients.  
2. **Corporates** — self-service travelers + travel managers, typically still connected to a TMC on the same platform (“shared platform for agents and travelers”).

### Geography & footprint
Public materials and press consistently cite offices in the **United States, United Kingdom, and Israel** (Portugal also appears in hiring/ops coverage). Sabre’s Feb 2024 release with Atriis lists **Southlake, Texas / Tel Aviv** as announcement locales and notes US/UK/Israel offices ([Sabre × Atriis press](https://www.atriis.com/press/sabre-and-atriis-sign-agreement-to-grow-corporate-ndc-bookings/)). Homepage footer lists **United States · United Kingdom · Israel** ([atriis.com](https://www.atriis.com/)).

### Founding & leadership (partially third-party)
- Company language: *“roots reaching back to a local travel company”* — SaaS spun from agency DNA ([company page](https://www.atriis.com/company/) where available).  
- Aggregators commonly list **~2016–2018** founding (LinkedIn/CB Insights/Crunchbase disagree by a year). **Atriis.com does not clearly publish a founding year on primary pages reviewed.**  
- Frequently cited leaders: **Omri Amsalem** (Co-founder; previously COO, more recently CEO in secondary sources), co-founders referenced around **Alon Meisels**, and product/tech leadership strengthened via the **2022 reTravel.io acquisition** (Nir Gurevich / Revital Kristal in trade coverage).

### Scale (estimates only)
Third-party estimates (LinkedIn / historical trade coverage around reTravel) suggest on the order of **tens of employees** and mid–low tens of millions USD revenue — **not confirmed by Atriis**. Treat as directional.

## 3. Global Shared Marketplace

Homepage framing: *“Scaling-up globally has never been easier — access local services, content and rates… Your TMC—without boundaries.”* ([atriis.com](https://www.atriis.com/))

**Intended idea:** Independent / regional TMCs on Atriis can tap **local content, rates, and services** from the network as corporate clients expand geographically — competing with mega-TMCs without building every local office.

**Public gap:** No dedicated marketplace product page with commercial mechanics (participation terms, revenue share, how a TMC joins) was found. Treat as a **strategic marketing concept** with incomplete public documentation.

---

## 4. Positioning narrative

Repeated across site and social:

1. **Leakage** — travelers book airline.com / OTAs / LCCs when the managed tool lacks content → lost TMC revenue and corporate policy visibility.  
2. **NDC *and* GDS** — not a binary; live comparison is the product story.  
3. **Defend the TMC** — tech that lets agencies stay relevant vs Navan/TravelPerk-style direct-to-corporate stacks.

Tone is **TMC-first** (agency economics + agent productivity), not pure consumer PLG.

**Atriis risks:** TMC consolidation shrinking independent base; funded DTC competitors; thin public documentation of marketplace economics; limited South Asia footprint.

## 11. Implications for FlightOne

FlightOne is a **Pakistan-origin leisure / custom-tour** business ([flightone.co](https://www.flightone.co/)), not a Western corporate TMC. **Atriis is not a natural near-term platform purchase.**

### Worth stealing as *ideas*
1. **Leakage framing** — every off-WhatsApp / OTA booking is leakage; design one interface that wins the booking.  
2. **Shared agent + customer view** — consultants and travellers see the same itinerary/price truth (reduces quote drift).  
3. **Content aggregation as moat** — stitch DMCs, hotels, transfers, visa helpers into one proposal/booking surface for B2B agents.  
4. **Partner-led growth** — Atriis scales via regional TMCs without VC land-grab; analogous to FlightOne enabling other Pakistani agencies later.  
5. **Duty of care lite** — group-tour traveler tracking/comms for adventure / remote itineraries.

### Poor fit as *vendor*
- Supply stack is **Amadeus/Sabre/Travelport + Western NDC/rail/hotel** — no evident **Pakistan / PIA / South Asia** content network.  
- Product DNA is **policy, approvals, expense, corporate compliance** — mostly irrelevant to leisure custom tours.  
- **Enterprise pricing** unlikely to match FlightOne volume economics vs building leaner internal tools.

**Recommendation:** use this report as **competitive literacy + product inspiration**, not an RFP shortlist item for FlightOne’s current roadmap.
